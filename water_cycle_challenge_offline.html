💧 The Water Cycle Challenge

Explore how water moves around our planet. Work together, discuss your ideas and complete the challenge.

1. Explore the diagram

Study the arrows and stages. Use the diagram to help your group answer the questions below.

Remember: the Sun provides energy, water vapour rises, clouds form, water falls as precipitation, and water collects in rivers, lakes and oceans.

2. Match each stage to its meaning

Choose the best description for each stage. Discuss your choices before submitting.

Your group's score will appear here.

3. Think and discuss

Challenge question: What do you think would happen to the water cycle if the Sun stopped heating water?

Take turns sharing an idea. Be ready to explain your group's thinking to the class.

Teacher note: This activity is designed to be saved on each tablet and opened offline. Learners can complete it in small groups during the Explore phase.
